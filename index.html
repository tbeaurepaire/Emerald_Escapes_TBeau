Welcome to Emerald Escapes

Freedom & Customization for Independent Travelers

Founded on a passion for authentic Irish discovery, Emerald Escapes was built for travelers who crave independence without the stress of planning everything from scratch. We believe every journey through Ireland should be as unique as the person taking it.

Whether you dream of winding through the rugged Wild Atlantic Way or cozying up in ancient village pubs, our customizable, self-drive itineraries give you total freedom behind the wheel with the peace of mind of expert local support.
